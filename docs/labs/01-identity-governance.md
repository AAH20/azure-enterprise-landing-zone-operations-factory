# Lab 1: Identity and governance

1. Model platform, networking, security, operations and application-owner groups.
2. Build a least-privilege RBAC matrix and identify privileged roles requiring just-in-time activation.
3. Preview the subscription policy and budget.
4. Inject a missing-tag resource and record the audit result.
5. Compare audit, deny, modify and deploy-if-not-exists effects.

Evidence: redacted identity matrix, policy assignment, compliance result, budget and ADR. Do not publish user email addresses or object IDs.
