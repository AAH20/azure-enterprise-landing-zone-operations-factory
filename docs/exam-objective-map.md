# AZ-104 and AZ-305 objective map

| Lab | Operational objective | Architecture objective | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity/governance | users, groups, RBAC, policy, subscriptions | identity and governance design | role matrix, policy result, ADR |
| Networking | VNets, peering, NSGs, routing, DNS | connectivity and network security | topology, effective routes, failure test |
| Compute/data | choose and administer compute/storage | compute, data and integration design | decision matrix, cost comparison |
| Operations/recovery | Monitor, backup, troubleshoot | continuity, observability and reliability | KQL, alert, restore evidence |
| Architecture review | maintain Azure resources | Well-Architected trade-offs | scored review and remediation backlog |

This map is a study aid, not a claim of Microsoft endorsement or certification.
